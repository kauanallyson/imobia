# WhatsApp through Meta's official Cloud API, directly

Imobia connects to WhatsApp through Meta's Cloud API, without a reseller (BSP) in between. Each Broker connects their own number through Embedded Signup, and coexistence lets them keep using the WhatsApp Business app on it, so replies they send from the app during a Takeover reach Imobia too. We rejected unofficial gateways (Evolution API, WAHA, Z-API): they're faster and cheaper to start with, but they break Meta's terms and put the Broker's number at risk of a ban. We rejected resellers to avoid their markup and lock-in to their API. The cost is that Imobia must be onboarded as a Meta Tech Provider (business verification and app review) and pays Meta's per-message fees. The Assistant serves the Broker's own customers, so it stays within Meta's 2026 rule against general-purpose AI chatbots.

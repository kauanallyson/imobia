# Imobia only sells developers' Lots and Off-plan Houses

For now, Imobia covers one business: a Broker selling units of developers' Developments, either Lots or Off-plan Houses, to buyers who reach out on WhatsApp. Rentals, owners offering their own property, and resale of finished houses are deliberately out of scope. That keeps a single sequence of Stages and lets every Property belong to a Development. Adding any of them back means revisiting the Stages, Availability and the Development–Property relationship, not just adding a property type.

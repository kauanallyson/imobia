# A Deal is one Contact and at most one Property

Most CRMs put one card per person on the kanban. We chose one Deal per Contact per Property, so a Contact interested in three Properties has three Deals, each in its own Stage, and a Contact who hasn't picked a Property yet has one Deal with none. A buyer can be at Visit for one Lot and only asking about another, and per-person cards would hide that. The cost is more cards on the board, and turning this back into per-person cards later would mean merging existing Deals.

Rules that keep this consistent: a Contact has at most one open Deal per Property and at most one open Deal with no Property. Picking a Property fills in the Property-less Deal, and each further Property starts a new Deal at Qualifying with the same Search Criteria. A Deal needs a Property from Visit on.
